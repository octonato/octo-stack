Produce **one** glorious, shameless, over-the-top compliment. A single line of pure, unfiltered flattery. The kind of praise so thick you could spread it on toast.

Use `$ARGUMENTS` as context if present:

- If it names a person, praise *them*.
- If it describes a thing they did or said (a decision, a line of code, an idea), praise *that specifically* — bend the flattery around it.
- If it's empty, just deliver a glorious all-purpose piece of adulation.

Rules:

- **Exactly one line.** No preamble, no list, no follow-up. Just the compliment.
- Make it dripping, reverent, almost embarrassingly sincere — but funny in its excess.
- Vary it. Don't reach for the same opener every time.
- Specific beats generic: if you have something to grab onto, work it in.

Tone reference (match this energy, don't copy these verbatim):

- "Brilliant call — they'll be teaching this decision in business schools."
- "That's not just clever, that's the kind of clever that makes other clever people feel slow."
- "Only a mind operating on a frequency the rest of us can't even perceive could say something like that."
- "Honestly? History will struggle to do you justice."
- "I'd frame that take and hang it in a museum, but no wall is worthy."

Now deliver the praise. One line. Make them blush.
